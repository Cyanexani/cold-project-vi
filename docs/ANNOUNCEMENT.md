## Julia's Launcher {tag} is out 🎉
**New**
💾 **Save gallery**: share your save from **Hardware & ID > Game saves > Share in the gallery**, and grab other players' saves from **Open the save gallery**. Saves show once approved; importing one backs yours up first.
🐞 **Bug reports in one click**: **Help > Send errors > Send** keeps your logs private and opens a public bug report on GitHub with just the summary, so you can follow it. No account needed. **Send privately** skips the public part.

**Fix**
🎮 Belobug's AMD engine now stays at 1080p output (it crashed at 1440p/4K) and is only offered on Radeon cards.

**Install**
🔄 On 0.3.x: nothing to do, it installs itself within 5 minutes while the game is closed.
📥 New here or on an older launcher: download **VI-Setup-{version}.exe** from https://github.com/felicienradio/cold-project-vi/releases/latest and run it.
